**I would not release this with an unqualified “CERTIFIED” claim yet.** There are definite wrong-phase labeling paths, export paths that trust summaries instead of certificates, and an unenforced foreign-solver convergence requirement.

This review cannot establish whether the particular 32 entries are valid: the result files and implementations of `ms_tfmin`, `ms_tfmin_hom`, `mintime_hypothesis_gates`, `run_capped`, the conjugate test, and the final catalog builder are not supplied. Findings below distinguish demonstrable code defects from obligations those missing implementations must discharge.

## SILENT-ACCEPTANCE — release blockers

- **[SILENT-ACCEPTANCE]** `run_dro_tulip.m:147-155,90` — **The departure walk can certify one phase and report another.** `round(1/abs(delta))` does not make `1/nD == abs(delta)`. This fails even on the advertised grid: starting at `sD=0`, requesting `5/12` produces `nD=2`, walks to `1/2`, and then line 90 labels the result `5/12`. The below-`tolPhase` skip can also relabel a nearby departure without re-solving it. **Fix:** make the walker accept an explicit unwrapped target phase, shorten its last step to that target, and certify against the exact requested endpoint. Never overwrite certified phase identity merely to match the request.

- **[SILENT-ACCEPTANCE]** `sheet_to_catalog_file.m:59-80` — **The export gate is not a certification gate.** A finite `S.TF(j)` sets `Q.OK=true` without locating any successful certificate or checking `S.Z8`. Ribs trust `P.ok` without validating its type or the numerical payload. In particular, `P.ok=true; P.z(8)=NaN` passes line 74 and can overwrite a valid entry because `existingTF <= NaN` is false. Inconsistent `S.TF` and `S.Z8(8,:)` are also shipped. **Fix:** export from validated certificate records, not summary arrays. Require finite, real, correctly sized data; positive time; exact problem/phase identity; and successful evidence under the release policy. Derive both time representations from `z(8)`.

- **[SILENT-ACCEPTANCE]** `sheet_to_catalog_file.m:46-63` — **The spine is assigned to the caller’s `sD0`, not its certified departure phase.** `idxOf(Q.sD,sD0)` essentially selects the first row by construction. A sheet built at `S.anc.sD=1/12`, packaged with defaults, is silently placed at departure zero. **Fix:** obtain the sheet’s departure from authoritative problem/certificate metadata, distinguish that from the output grid origin, and require a unique matching grid row. Reject disagreement among `S.anc.sD`, candidate phases, and endpoint identity.

- **[SILENT-ACCEPTANCE]** `sheet_to_catalog_file.m:52,85-91` — **Packaging can relabel an existing trajectory with a different engine or orbit.** Thrust, Isp, initial mass, and orbit parameters come from fresh options/defaults, not the problem that was certified. No equality check binds them to `S.B` or the ribs. **Fix:** stamp each certificate with a versioned problem identity containing propulsion, units, orbit reconstruction and phase origins, endpoint conventions, and model version. Derive export metadata from that identity; permit options only to assert matching values, not replace them.

- **[SILENT-ACCEPTANCE]** `build_ribs.m:40-53`; `rib_from_crossing.m:53` — **Ribs need not belong to the loaded sheet’s problem.** The builder reconstructs the default setup instead of using or validating the sheet’s setup; the walker starts at `anc.sD`, not `C0.sD`. A nondefault sheet can therefore acquire ribs generated at a different operating point or on a different departure grid. Those results may be valid for their actual endpoints but are not valid additions to the original sheet. **Fix:** reconstruct from the sheet’s problem identity and assert `C0`, `B`, and `anc` agree before walking. Store that identity in every saved rib.

- **[SILENT-ACCEPTANCE]** `certify_root.m:105-118,146-162` — **Malformed verdicts and diagnostics can pass.** Examples:
  - `it.converged=true` bypasses any independent check of `it.normR`, including `NaN` or a large value.
  - `it.conj.pass=[]` makes `if C.conj ~= 1` false in MATLAB.
  - Empty hypothesis fields can similarly bypass rejection conditions.
  - `g.minLamV=Inf` and `g.minQmt=Inf` pass positivity tests.
  - Mixed vector verdicts can defeat scalar-looking `if` conditions.
  
  **Fix:** validate every external result before interpreting it: expected schema, real finite scalars, and scalar success flags equal to the documented success value. Enforce the residual bound regardless of `it.converged`. Apply the same validation to `okC/okF/okW/okG`.

- **[SILENT-ACCEPTANCE]** `certify_root.m:135-143` — **The foreign witness need not have converged.** Agreement of the returned vector with the input does not prove a successful independent solve. A solver that returns its initial guess on stagnation or iteration exhaustion gives `dz=0` and passes. The header’s “MUST converge” is not enforced by the supplied caller. **Fix:** obtain and require the foreign solver’s documented convergence status and final residual. If its API supplies no status, add a checked adapter around it. Independently evaluate the returned solution’s complete boundary/PMP residual; retain `dz <= 1e-6` as an additional agreement test.

- **[SILENT-ACCEPTANCE]** `certify_root.m:122-129` — **A returned flight array is assumed to be a completed, feasible flight.** The time output is discarded, so an integrator that stops early without throwing is not explicitly rejected. There is no direct check of positive time, positive mass, or mass-flow consistency. **Fix:** require integration success and `t(end)=z(8)` within a declared tolerance. For this normalized all-burn model, enforce
  \[
  t_f>0,\qquad m(t)=1-\frac{T_{\rm nd}}{c_{\rm nd}}t,\qquad m(t_f)>0,
  \]
  together with finite trajectory data and any additional state-domain constraints the model claims. Check these before accepting flight-derived metrics.

- **[SILENT-ACCEPTANCE]** `build_arrival_sheet.m:67-74` — **Configured certification policy applies to crossings but not library seeds.** Seeds are certified with a fresh hardcoded options struct; `opts.copts` is used only for crossings. For example, requesting a stricter flown-position gate does not apply it to seeds, which can nevertheless populate the same “certified” sheet. **Fix:** construct one validated certification policy, attach the pool once, and use it for both routes. Record its identifier and tolerances in every result and in the sheet.

- **[SILENT-ACCEPTANCE]** `sheet_from_arcs.m:67-76,92-117` — **Pre-certified seeds and injected certifier results are accepted solely on `C.ok`.** Neither the candidate’s departure/operating point nor its returned arrival identity is checked. Seeds are mapped to a canonical grid phase but are not necessarily certified against that exact phase; crossings, in contrast, are re-solved at the canonical phase. **Fix:** validate certificate identity and policy at insertion, require the returned phase to match the requested cell, and re-certify seeds at canonical endpoints when necessary. Keep arbitrary `certFn` injection in an explicitly non-release/test mode unless its result passes the same validator.

- **[SILENT-ACCEPTANCE]** `package_phase_catalog.m:57-64` — **A persistent staging directory plus a wildcard can ship stale, unaudited sheets.** Existing matching files remain in `phase_sheet_<tag>` and are consumed along with the newly written file. **Fix:** package from a fresh private staging directory or an explicit file list. Verify that the final catalog has a bijective correspondence with the validated input entries, including problem identity, phases, costates, and times. Do not infer release contents from a glob.

## CORRECTNESS — mathematics and continuation

- **[CORRECTNESS]** `certify_root.m:149-168`; `package_phase_catalog.m:65-70` — **The visible checks do not constitute a rigorous continuous-time sufficiency certificate.** Finite sampled minima do not prove positivity between samples; a thresholded numerical rank does not prove `dim S=1`; and a numerical conjugate verdict needs error-controlled exclusion of missed zeros and endpoint degeneracy. The missing gate implementations might supply stronger guarantees, but this interface neither requires nor preserves them. **Fix:** either describe the output as *numerically certified under a specified policy*, or provide validated root/error bounds and continuous-time bounds supporting the cited sufficiency theorem. Preserve margins, uncertainty estimates, singular-value gaps, and the precise theorem/boundary conditions—not just three numbers and a verdict.

- **[CORRECTNESS]** `sheet_from_arcs.m:115-117`; `build_ribs.m:45-53`; `run_dro_tulip.m:130-137` — **“Minimum time” must not imply a global or free-phase optimum.** The sheet minimum is only over discovered successful candidates. Ribs launch from only the fastest spine candidate, although a slower spine branch can become faster at another departure. The front door selects only the last converged arrival crossing. No branch-completeness argument is present. **Fix:** state explicitly that these are fixed-phase local candidates and “fastest found” values. For broader coverage, trace all relevant certified branches and both senses, and certify all target crossings. If phases themselves are optimization variables, their transversality conditions must also be included; continuation in a phase does not enforce them.

- **[CORRECTNESS]** `arclength_arrival.m:68-69,164-170` — **The analytical derivative is correct for the exact orbit, but not exactly for the implemented spline target.** With the stated residual structure,
  \[
  \frac{\partial R_{\rm term}}{\partial s_A}
  =-T_A f_{\rm CR3BP}(x_A(s_A))
  \]
  is correct when \(x_A\) is the ballistic flow. Here `x_A` is a componentwise cubic spline, whose derivative generally differs from the field evaluated at the spline state. Ordinary splines also do not enforce periodic \(C^1\) matching across `mod(s,1)`. **Fix:** differentiate the same interpolation representation used by the residual, or use an orbit representation with a consistent derivative. Enforce periodic closure and derivative matching, verify the time origin/period convention, and test `dRdq` against finite differences of the actual residual, including the seam.

- **[CORRECTNESS]** `arclength_ms.m:137-147,203-204` — **Rank regularity is reported but not enforced.** A right-null vector from a rank-deficient augmented matrix can have a tiny `tanResid` while not defining a unique tangent. The fold ratio does not establish that the augmented matrix is numerically regular. Moreover, rank loss of `R_x` plus augmented regularity identifies a projection critical point, not necessarily a nondegenerate simple fold. **Fix:** require augmented row rank with a scale-aware singular-value gap before continuing. At a classified simple fold, require `rank(R_x)=n-1` and a nonzero turning curvature, equivalently the appropriate left-null-vector/Hessian nondegeneracy test. Otherwise report a degenerate or unresolved event.

- **[CORRECTNESS]** `arclength_ms.m:174-210` — **“Fold localized” currently means only “some corrected point lies on the root curve.”** `cvF` becomes true after any successful plane correction and remains true when eight false-position iterations are exhausted or a later correction fails. There is no final requirement that `tau_q` be near zero, yet `haveTurn=cvF` uses that point as the segment extremum. This can mis-bracket precisely the near-fold levels the code intends to recover. **Fix:** maintain separate root-correction and event-localization statuses. Use safeguarded bracketing with a tangent/event tolerance and a phase uncertainty bound; reject/subdivide the continuation step when the extremum cannot be resolved sufficiently for nearby levels.

- **[CORRECTNESS]** `arclength_ms.m:225-239` — **A requested level equal to the internal turning-point level is omitted.** On the first half, `L==qb` is excluded because it is not the last half. On the second, `L==qa` is excluded by the strict-product test. Thus the fold-level root is recorded zero times, not once. **Fix:** define explicit ownership of every endpoint, including an internal turning point, and record a level contact there once. Use event uncertainty/tolerances rather than relying solely on floating-point equality.

- **[CORRECTNESS]** `arclength_ms.m:164,223-245,322-360` — **The implementation cannot guarantee “every crossing,” especially after folds.** Two turns within one accepted step can leave endpoint tangent signs unchanged. Even when a turn is found, unrestricted fixed-\(q\) Newton can jump to the other branch; both halves may return the same root and lose the second crossing. The fold corrector likewise has no locality guard. **Fix:** impose curvature/tangent-rotation-based subdivision, localize level events in an arclength bracket, and preserve branch identity during correction. Share admissibility, damping, and correction-distance safeguards across all correctors. Unless interval/uniqueness bounds are supplied, document crossing enumeration as resolution-dependent rather than exhaustive.

- **[CORRECTNESS]** `certify_root.m:100-101,149-162` — **The theorem must apply to the full admissible control problem, not merely the restricted all-burn ODE.** The supplied files do not expose the minimized Hamiltonian, costate equations, terminal mass transversality, or definitions of `Q_mt` and `S`. Consequently their correspondence to the claimed sufficiency theorem is unaudited. **Fix:** document and test that correspondence. For the usual minimum-Hamiltonian convention, an all-burn justification can use
  \[
  H=\rho+\lambda_r^\top v+\lambda_v^\top(g+h)
     -T_{\rm nd}\!\left(\frac{\|\lambda_v\|}{m}+\frac{\lambda_m}{c_{\rm nd}}\right).
  \]
  With positive mass, \(\lambda_m(t_f)=0\), and the correct adjoint equation, \(\lambda_m(t)\ge0\); nonzero \(\lambda_v\) then gives strict preference for full throttle. Verify those premises rather than treating “all burn” alone as proof.

## ROBUSTNESS — numerical reliability and failure handling

- **[ROBUSTNESS]** `certify_root.m:109-118,165-166` — **The plateau policy is defensible only with additional controls.** The code calls *every* nonconverged result below `tolRelax` a plateau, potentially including an in-process time limit or another failure status. More importantly, a small residual need not imply a small solution error near an ill-conditioned shooting Jacobian. A foreign solver agreeing with the same approximate root and a 100 km/10 m/s flight gate do not establish the missing error bound. **Fix:** whitelist genuinely benign stagnation statuses; require finite residuals; estimate corrections/forward error using the scaled Jacobian; and compare numerical uncertainty with sufficiency margins. Record the actual termination status. Hard-cap failures already take a separate rejection path and must remain so.

- **[ROBUSTNESS]** `arclength_ms.m:135-141,263-278`; `arclength_arrival.m:119-128` — **Unknown scaling is implemented; residual scaling is not.** Comments call the merit function “scaled,” but it is `[R; planeResidual]` with raw continuity, terminal, Hamiltonian, and normalization rows. Their relative weights affect Newton backtracking, rank diagnostics, and tolerance interpretation. **Fix:** introduce explicit residual scales and apply them consistently to residuals, Jacobians, merit functions, and rank tests. Keep physical residual diagnostics separately. The `sqrt(K)` unknown scaling is a sensible equal-weight RMS approximation, but use quadrature weights for nonuniform meshes and verify refinement stability. Initial sphere normalization does not bound all interior costates.

- **[ROBUSTNESS]** `arclength_ms.m:92-125,134-137` — **Basic numerical contracts are unchecked.** All-zero `p0` makes the default `Dx` identically zero. Negative/nonfinite scales, inconsistent dimensions, invalid step bounds, or a non-scalar direction are accepted until a later failure. `norm(R,inf)>1e-6` also does not reject a NaN start residual, and the start is not checked for admissibility. **Fix:** validate real finite input shapes, positive scales, integer limits, `direction∈{-1,+1}`, and ordered step/range bounds. Require a finite admissible start satisfying an explicit start tolerance. Validate `R`, `J`, and `dRdq` dimensions at every external boundary.

- **[ROBUSTNESS]** `certify_root.m:80-88,173-184`; `capped_pool.m:31-39` — **The “hard fence” is conditional, and `parfeval` cancellation alone is not a demonstrated hard process bound.** Missing pools explicitly select unfenced execution. Existing pools are reused without checking whether their type supports the intended isolation. `run_capped` is absent, so its timeout, cancellation, late-result, and worker-recovery semantics cannot be audited. A noninterruptible native call may not stop merely because cancellation was requested. **Fix:** require a supported process-based execution environment in certified production mode. For a true hard kill guarantee, supervise an independently terminable process. Test timeout and worker-error paths, and never accept late outputs after timeout.

- **[ROBUSTNESS]** `arclength_arrival.m:65,84,99-111,159`; `arclength_ms.m:124,178,239,261` — **Many expensive calls are outside any fence.** Creating a pool in the caller does not fence endpoint construction, anchor reconstruction/polishing, continuation residual evaluations, or crossing/fold correction. These can still hang before certification is reached. **Fix:** route all potentially grinding external evaluations through a checked execution layer, or fence the complete continuation/setup task while periodically checkpointing accepted results. Narrow the documentation if the guarantee is intended to cover only certification.

- **[ROBUSTNESS]** `arclength_ms.m:132,174-245,254-301`; `run_dro_tulip.m:128,135,150-151` — **The advertised wall budget is not an end-to-end budget.** Continuation checks it only between accepted steps; fold searches and retry loops can run far beyond it. The front door independently reuses the full budget for continuation, certification, and departure walking. **Fix:** propagate one absolute deadline and give each operation `min(stageCap,remainingTime)`. Check it inside retries and event searches. Add total-solve/substep limits to the rib walker; `maxBisect` is not a total work budget because successful steps decrement its counter.

- **[ROBUSTNESS]** `certify_root.m:122-129,173-184` — **External failure handling is inconsistent.** Flight exceptions can escape instead of producing the promised named failure; malformed returned data can also throw outside the existing `try` blocks. Timeout and worker error are inconsistently conflated in messages. **Fix:** use a common external-call result structure with distinct `success`, `timeout`, `exception`, and `invalidOutput` statuses, elapsed time, and exception identifiers. Fail closed on all of them; let the campaign decide whether an infrastructure/programming failure should abort rather than masquerade as an infeasible candidate.

- **[ROBUSTNESS]** `arclength_arrival.m:140-142` — **Default reverse continuation stops immediately.** With `direction=-1` and no `sAStop`, the default `inf` produces `qStop=[inf inf]`; every finite start is outside it. **Fix:** default to `[-inf inf]`, applying a one-sided finite bound only when requested, or make the default stopping phase direction-dependent.

- **[ROBUSTNESS]** `arclength_ms.m:125-128,304-307`; `run_dro_tulip.m:132` — **Some failure paths destroy the intended partial-result contract.** A deadline or invalid Jacobian before the first recorded root leaves `A.q` empty, and the final logger indexes `A.q(1)`. The front door also indexes `A.q(end)` on failure. Early start rejection leaves `A.nCalls` incorrect. **Fix:** finalize all exits through one routine that handles empty trajectories, updates counters, and returns a structured stop status without throwing during logging.

- **[ROBUSTNESS]** `run_dro_tulip.m:119-121`; `arclength_arrival.m:75-79`; `dro_tulip_library.m:47-57` — **Walking from a sweep-library entry uses an unsupported file layout.** Sweep entries refer to a file containing `S`, but setup only understands top-level `z/it.Y` or `best.z/best.it.Y`. The selected sweep cell cannot be recovered by reopening that file as an anchor. **Fix:** pass the selected seed record—`z`, `Y`, mesh, phases, and problem identity—directly to setup. A filename alone is not a root identifier.

- **[ROBUSTNESS]** `sheet_from_arcs.m:100-110` — **A failed attempt can suppress a later successful certificate.** A candidate failing a late gate already has finite `z` and `tfDays`. If another arc produces the same root and now certifies, duplicate detection discards the success because the earlier failure matches. It also contradicts the promise to retain verdicts. **Fix:** separate root identity from certification attempts. Store all attempts, promote a validated successful attempt over failures, and recompute summaries from successful records.

- **[ROBUSTNESS]** `run_dro_tulip.m:130-137` — **The last converged arrival crossing is not necessarily the usable one.** It may fail certification while an earlier crossing passes, or it may be slower. **Fix:** certify all converged crossings of the requested level and retain their outcomes. Select according to the documented policy; when departure walking remains, preserve alternative branches rather than assuming the fastest intermediate point gives the fastest final transfer.

- **[ROBUSTNESS]** `certify_crossing.m:56-70` — **Chart unpacking assumes the layout without validating it.** `rho=p(end)`, the time index, and the junction slice are trusted even if `anc` and `p` come from different meshes/layouts. `rho=Inf` passes the cutoff. Also, a small positive multiplier is not mathematically abnormal; it is a poorly conditioned normal-chart conversion. **Fix:** validate finite data, expected length, `nExtra==1`, the exact time/rho indices, and mesh consistency. Distinguish `rho==0`, inadmissible sign, and an engineering conditioning cutoff in failure reasons.

- **[ROBUSTNESS]** `build_arrival_sheet.m:44-48`; `arclength_arrival.m:137-149` — **Saved arcs and supplied anchors are not checked against the reconstructed continuation problem.** Globbed arcs may have another departure, mesh, operating point, or residual layout. Arc mode rebuilds `B` from options but keeps the caller’s `anc`. Re-certification can rescue a seed for the current problem, but then the stored branch/crossing provenance is false. **Fix:** validate problem and layout identities before tracing or assembling; make the problem object an explicit argument rather than reconstructing it implicitly.

- **[ROBUSTNESS]** `package_phase_catalog.m:48,59-76`; `sheet_to_catalog_file.m:68-72` — **Release input handling is too permissive and partially inconsistent.** Requested missing rib files are skipped; geometry-bearing filenames/descriptions remain `tau1/Np7` despite configurable metadata; and a non-scalar rib struct array is wrapped into one cell rather than normalized element-by-element. **Fix:** fail on missing requested inputs in release mode, generate names/descriptions from validated metadata, normalize rib arrays explicitly, and record a complete input manifest. Add a round-trip test that reconstructs and checks every exported `OK` entry—not merely its count.

- **[ROBUSTNESS]** `conjugate_pole_predict.m:80-101,107-118` — **The reported fit success is weak even as a diagnostic.** The pole equation matches only the first and last increments; interior samples affect the reported least-squares residual, but that residual is never gated. The fixed bracket can miss a pole despite `maxAhead=inf`. Conditioning growth also does not identify a conjugate point without excluding chart/scaling and other singularities. **Fix:** fit all observations, require a residual/uncertainty criterion, expand a physically bounded bracket, and name the output a conditioning-pole warning. Move this unused helper to diagnostics; do not treat it as certification evidence.

## Correct as written, subject to the stated residual conventions

- **Homogeneous → normal conversion:** `certify_crossing.m:65-70` scales exactly the right components: all seven initial costates and junction rows **8:14**, including \(\lambda_m\). Junction rows **1:7**, physical mass, and \(t_f\) must not scale. For finite \(\rho>0\), positive scaling preserves the minimizing control and gives \(H_N=H_H/\rho=0\); the homogeneous normalization constraint is discarded in the normal chart.

- **CR3BP field and derivative placement:** `cr3bp_field.m:26-33` has the standard rotating-frame signs. The minus sign and six terminal rows in `dRdsA` are right under the supplied “only terminal matching depends on arrival phase” premise. The problem is derivative consistency with the spline, not the physical formula.

- **Tangent orientation through a fold:** full SVD of the \(n\times(n+1)\) augmented Jacobian supplies the missing right-null vector; orienting successive tangents by their dot product is appropriate. **Do not reset the tangent to the original phase direction after a fold.**

- **Several existing rejection paths are genuinely fail-closed:** scalar NaN flown misses and witness differences are rejected; a scalar conjugate verdict other than `1` is rejected; false capped-call statuses are rejected. The defects are missing result validation, missing witness convergence semantics, and downstream trust—not those particular comparisons.
