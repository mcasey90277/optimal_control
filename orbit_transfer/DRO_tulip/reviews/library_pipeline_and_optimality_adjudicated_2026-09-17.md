# The DRO -> tulip library-generation pipeline and its local-optimality gates: GPT-6 Astra (xhigh) + two host forks, adjudicated (2026-09-17)

**Scope.** Everything under `run_phase_torus` (adjudicated separately in `run_phase_torus_review2_adjudicated_2026-09-17.md`): the chain script `build_70mN_library`, the front door `indirect/run_costate_library`, arcs (`arclength_arrival`, `arclength_ms`, `ms_tfmin_hom`, `crossings_from_arc`, `certify_crossing`), the sheet (`build_arrival_sheet`, `sheet_from_arcs`), ribs (`rib_from_crossing`, `build_ribs`, `rib_validate`, `rib_targets`), packaging (`sheet_to_catalog_file`, `package_phase_catalog`, `audit_phase_catalog`), `family_map`, the filler (`fill_holes_direct`, `direct_cell_solve`, `harvest_ms_seed`), the full gate stack (`certify_root`, `ms_tfmin`, `ms_bvp`, `ms_conjugate_test`, `mintime_hypothesis_gates`, `lift_space_dim`, `lift_margin`, `pmp_pointwise_checks`, `conj_spectrum`), the orbit providers (`get_family_orbit`, `survey_family_bounds`), and four documents (`doc/mintime_second_order_audit.tex`, `doc/extremal_and_local_min_survey.md`, `doc/transfer_problem_space.md`, `DRO_tulip/doc/phase_torus_methods.tex`). Three questions: Q1 pipeline correctness as a system; Q2 sufficiency of the local-optimality tests; Q3 generalisation to any orbit pair.

**Runs.** Astra: raw API, xhigh, 439 KB bundle, HTTP 200 in 546 s, 137,514 prompt / 23,243 completion tokens (11,307 reasoning), $2.88; transcript `library_pipeline_and_optimality_astra_2026-09-17.md`. Host fork A read the gate stack against the audit document; host fork B read the pipeline for correctness and for its DRO/tulip inventory. Neither fork ran MATLAB. The host verified each adjudicated claim below at the cited lines; MATLAB semantics were checked in the shared R2026a session where stated.

Verdicts: **CONFIRMED** (mechanism reproduced at the cited lines), **PLAUSIBLE** (consistent, consequence not demonstrated), **DISAGREE** (with reason). Where two or three reviewers found the same defect independently it is marked so; independent convergence is the strongest evidence this pass produced.

## Q1. Pipeline correctness

### P0: loses roots, mislabels a campaign, or blocks an advertised configuration

1. **CONFIRMED (Astra + both forks + driver review). The whole pipeline is set up at the shipped operating point.** `run_costate_library.m:239-243` passes the sheet builder no `sD`, `anchorMat` or `sA0`; `build_ribs` and `fill_holes_direct` reload and re-polish the default 70 mN anchor (`arclength_arrival.m:113, 139`). Any campaign with `sD(1) ~= 0`, another engine, or another orbit pair fails after its arcs have been walked, or polishes the wrong anchor. **Fix:** `physicsOnly = true` wherever only closures are needed (ribs, filler, discovery); thread `sD(1)`, `anchorMat`, `sA0` from the driver into the sheet call. This is also the first step of Q3.

2. **CONFIRMED (Astra + fork B). The single-departure-phase case is blocked unconditionally.** `run_costate_library.m:275-278` sets `cols = []` when `nPts == 0`, and `:304-308` returns `blocked` on an empty `cols`. `phase_torus_examples` example 1 cannot run. **Fix:** guard the block with `nPts > 0`; package through the no-rib path.

3. **CONFIRMED (Astra). The catalog audit fails open.** A polish that fails or times out leaves `conjNow = NaN`; a failed hypothesis call leaves `dimSNow = NaN`; every rejection is written as `isfinite(x) && x ~= ...`, so a missing result is a clean row (`audit_phase_catalog.m:133-149`). Successful hypothesis output is not checked for `minLamV`, `minQmt`, H6 or the lift margin, the dense scan and pointwise stack are not repeated, and the polished root is discarded so a moved root is not detected. The library-of-record audits (576/0 on the 24 x 24 torus) were run through this instrument. **Fix:** fail closed (a missing result is BAD), repeat the full production policy, compare the polished root to the stored one. Re-run the audit on the 24 x 24 library once fixed and record the count.

4. **CONFIRMED (Astra). Rescanning replaces the walk's fold-aware crossings with a scan that cannot see inside a step.** The walker splits a step at a resolved turning point and brackets each half (`arclength_ms.m:268-307`); `crossings_from_arc.m:108-130` brackets only adjacent stored points and `build_arrival_sheet.m:74-76` overwrites `A.crossings` with its result. The turning-point state `pTurn` is not persisted in `A.folds`. A level crossed twice inside one ~2e-4 step beside a fold, exactly where a column earns its second candidate, is lost on every rebuilt sheet. Rare (the level must fall within one step of the fold) but silent. **Fix:** persist `(qTurn, pTurn)` in the fold record and split in the rescanner too; until then merge rather than replace. The header claim "the re-scan reproduces the recorded crossings exactly" was true of the 24 x 24 arcs by luck of where the folds fell; `test_crossings_from_arc` has no fold-adjacent double-crossing case. Add one.

5. **CONFIRMED (Astra). The front door's own default grid is refused by its own check.** `sA = sA0 + (0:nA-1)/nA` (`run_costate_library.m:180`) ends at 1.0337; `checkPhaseList` requires every phase below 1. Only callers that pass explicit `sort(mod(...))` lists (the driver, the examples) get through. **Fix:** canonicalise the default with `sort(mod(., 1))`.

6. **CONFIRMED (Astra + fork B). Every dimensional mass in a certificate assumes 150 kg.** `certify_root.m:127` defaults `m0kg = 150`; no caller passes it; `propellantKg`, `finalMassKg` (`:252-253`) are wrong for any other `m0`. Length and time scales are literals at `:188`. **Fix:** read them from `B.problem`; treat options as assertions against it.

7. **CONFIRMED (fork B). Non-default orbits are mislabelled and the pictures stage cannot find its file.** `package_phase_catalog.m:83` stages `dro_tulip_<tag>_tau1_Np7.mat` for every problem while `build_70mN_library.m:360` loads `tau%g_Np%d`; the description text is likewise literal. **Fix:** build both from `S.problem`.

8. **CONFIRMED (fork B). `build_70mN_library` drops `pmTulip` from `setupOpts`** (`:154-155`), so a manual chain run at `pm = +1` builds the mirror problem; masked when driven through the front door. Astra adds three more dropped interfaces in the same script (anchor phases before overrides, `arcDirUsed` not passed to the sheet, ribs walked with `1/12` for a 24-row request); PLAUSIBLE, not re-read line by line.

### P1: wrong reuse, wrong identity, wasted compute

9. **CONFIRMED (Astra + fork B + driver review). Identity by t_f alone, three places.** `family_map.m:104, 240-267` attaches a spine root to a family within 0.02 d (the methods document says 1e-5 d), rib matching allows 0.03 d; the registry dedups at 1e-3 d; `sheet_to_catalog_file.m:126` picks the winner by `TF` within 1e-9 although distinct equal-time roots are supported elsewhere. Only `sheet_from_arcs.m:105-121` compares z8. **Fix:** one root-identity contract (phase + normal-chart `lam0` direction), a stored winner index, and family lineage carried by root ID rather than recovered from time.

10. **CONFIRMED (Astra). `arclength_ms` fold localisation: a first-iteration exact hit leaves `tqLast = NaN`.** Initialised NaN at `:192`, assigned at `:212` only after the `abs(tq) < 1e-10` break at `:211`, so `resolved` is false, the step is not split and the fold is not classified. Rare. **Fix:** assign before the break. Astra's second point, that `10*sminX/sminAug` is not an event-location error bound, is PLAUSIBLE and should be re-derived when the fold tolerance is next touched.

11. **CONFIRMED (fork B, Astra concurs). A seed within 1e-6 of a list phase is certified at its own phase and filed at the list phase** (`build_arrival_sheet.m:106-116`, `sheet_from_arcs.m:322-328`). On the tulip that is ~2 km of endpoint, above the audit's 1 km gate. Latent (registry roots are exact list values). **Fix:** certify at the matched list value.

12. **CONFIRMED (Astra). The rib walker takes the shortest wrapped path, not the directed offset `rib_targets` chose** (`rib_from_crossing.m:98-104, 130`), and its trial step is `1/nD`, not the list spacing (fork B). For a sparse list the rib reaches the phase by a different continuation path, possibly another branch, and pays up to eight refused certifications per target before advancing. **Fix:** keep an unwrapped `cur`, use `target - cur`, and derive the first step from the target spacing capped at 1/24.

13. **CONFIRMED (Astra). `multiplicity` is validated but not enforced.** `certify_root.m:462` builds `consistent` from `nZero`, `nUnresolved`, `nInterior` only, and acceptance is `CS.clear`; `multiplicity > 0` with the other counts zero certifies. Whether `conj_spectrum` can emit that combination is a contract question; the consumer should not depend on it. **Fix:** add `multiplicity == 0` to the consistency expression.

14. **CONFIRMED (Astra). Two diagnostics are computed and never consumed:** `fdAgree` (the finite-difference adjoint check's own agreement, `pmp_pointwise_checks.m:137`) is not in the certifier's `pwNeed` list, and `specConsistency` is not read. **Fix:** gate or label advisory.

15. **CONFIRMED (Astra). The test seam can ship.** A non-empty `override` only warns (`certify_root.m:170`); `C.ok` can be true and the certificate does not record the override. **Fix:** force diagnostic-only status when any override is present.

16. **CONFIRMED (Astra). `rib_validate` accepts a rib whose `.z` exists but is not a finite 8-vector, does not check each point's `.sA`, and its `abs(a-b) > tol` identity test is false for NaN** (`rib_validate.m:59-98`). Coverage is count-based. **Fix:** validate domain first (as `certify_root` does), compare the target-index set.

17. **PLAUSIBLE (Astra).** The direct filler's resume loads `R`/`rec` without checking the saved `problem` and re-stamps everything with the current one; a filled cell is never re-attempted even when a faster neighbour appears (`fill_holes_direct.m:109-125, 118-119`). Mechanism consistent with the code; not reproduced.

18. **PLAUSIBLE (Astra).** `crossings_from_arc` terminal-hit ownership is direction-dependent and `nWrap = 3` bounds the wrap search; `family_map` labels every fold candidate a fold regardless of `classified` and takes `abs(rho)`, erasing passage into the negative-multiplier chart. Worth a fixture each.

19. **CONFIRMED (Astra) MATLAB semantics.** `for sf = seedFiles` iterates over columns, so a column cell processes one file (`build_arrival_sheet.m:98`; the driver passes a 1 x 1 cell, so latent); default arguments are evaluated before the call, so `pickTStar(S)` can throw even when `tStarSec` is supplied and `capped_pool()` starts a pool even when one is passed.

20. **CONFIRMED (fork B) EFFICIENCY.** With `rescan = true` each arc's own start point is found as a crossing and certified again beside the identical library seed (~2.5 min per arc per round).

21. **DISAGREE in part (Astra).** "Physics reconstruction unnecessarily depends on the default anchor" is the same root as item 1, listed twice; and "only `outDir` is made absolute" is true but every campaign path in use is absolute, so it is hygiene, not a defect.

## Q2. Are the local-optimality tests sufficient?

Astra and fork A converge, independently and in detail, on one verdict: **the stack is a substantial numerical assessment of fixed-phase PMP extremals, not a certificate of strict strong local optimality.** The gap has five parts, in decreasing order of what it would take to close:

| # | gap | who found it | status | closable how |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a | **Fixed-phase scope.** Endpoints are fixed points (`ms_tfmin.m:145`); nothing tests optimality with respect to sliding either phase along its orbit, which is the question the catalog answers. A saddle of the free-phase problem passes by construction. | Astra, fork A | CONFIRMED | First order now: the sensitivities `dt_f/ds_A = tau_A lambda_rv(t_f) . f_orb(x_f)`, `dt_f/ds_D = -tau_D lambda_rv(0) . f_orb(x_0)` use quantities every entry carries; cross-check against the sheet's own central differences (fork A's test 2). Second order: a free-phase accessory problem with endpoint tangent and curvature terms (Astra, separate feature). |
| b | **H2/H3 are sampled positivity with no margin.** `minLamV > 0`, `minQmt > 0` (`certify_root.m:380-381`) pass 1e-300 and pass values below the measured lambda_m uncertainty (1e-6); no between-sample bound. FINDINGS 30's "1e-6 gate" does not exist in the certifier. | Astra, fork A | CONFIRMED | Lipschitz bound per interval (fork A): `min >= sampled min - max|d/dt| Delta t / 2`, gate the bound above the uncertainty. Astra supplies the exact gap identity `H(s,alpha) - H(1,alpha*) = T Q_mt (1-s) + (s T |lambda_v| / 2m) |alpha - alpha*|^2`, which is the quantitative strict-bang/Weierstrass statement once both coefficients have uniform lower bounds. |
| c | **H5 (no conjugate point) is assessed, not proved.** The dense scan is candidate-triggered sampling with a policy floor (`conj_spectrum.m:47-48`), `tUncovered` is stored and never gated (`certify_root.m:464-468`), and the short-time sign is never derived. | Astra, fork A | CONFIRMED | Analytic leading-order sign at t -> 0+ compared with the first trusted sample (fork A test 3); sigma_6 at integrator steps with a step-wise Lipschitz bound (test 4); validated enclosures only if a theorem is wanted. |
| d | **The theorem is not cited.** "BCT 2007 Thm. 3.x" is not an identifiable statement; the reduction from throttle-constrained to smooth direction-control second variation is asserted (audit `sec:reductions`), and the mixed bang/smooth sufficiency pattern (Maurer-Osmolovskii-Buskens) that the argument actually uses is unnamed. `mintime_hypothesis_gates.m:19` still says "no abnormal lift iff dim S = 1", which the corrected audit denies (the converse fails without the PMP cone inequalities). | Astra, fork A | CONFIRMED | One paragraph in the audit; fix the header. |
| e | **The lift margin bounds one error component.** Both constraint builds share the flight and interpolation; changing the adjoint tolerance does not bound their common error (`lift_margin.m:77-102`). A rank assessment, not an exclusion of continuous abnormal lifts. | Astra, fork A | CONFIRMED | Refine flight and interpolation independently; for rigour, bound the total matrix error and require sigma_6 above it. |

Both reviewers answer the direct question the same way: **yes, a saddle can pass** (through a missed conjugacy, the uncovered prefix, or the wrong endpoint variation space) and **a non-strict extremal can pass** (an exact degeneracy treated numerically as nonsingular). Conversely, under a correctly cited theorem with uniform margins, the correct accessory problem and established absence of conjugate points, those cases are excluded; the gap is between that conditional statement and finite sampled measurements. Fork A's other point stands: the mass costate is treated consistently throughout (excluded from the block, in the kernel identities and the lift space, `lambda_m(t_f) = 0` gated, H6 on `lambda_m(0)`); no sign or normalisation defect.

**Tests to add, merged and ordered** (fork A 1-6 and Astra 1-7 overlap almost entirely):
1. Fail-closed mutation suite through the audit and the certifier: every stage forced to fail, time out, return NaN, return a different root; `multiplicity > 0` with clear counts; non-empty override; uncovered startup. (Astra 1; the house rule from FINDINGS 2026-09-17.)
2. Lipschitz margins for H2/H3 above the measured uncertainty (fork A 1, Astra 4).
3. Phase-transversality cross-check against the sheet (fork A 2, Astra Q2 finding 1).
4. Short-time sign of the reduced determinant (fork A 3).
5. Independent accessory-problem inertia test: form the second variation with the actual terminal conditions, compare its degeneracies with `[Phi_rv P, F_rv]` (Astra 2). This is the test that would make the audit's Prop. 1 a checked statement rather than a derivation.
6. Variational-flow validation independent of the shared STM: AD or scaled perturbations, fixtures with an early conjugate point, a pair inside one coarse interval, an even-multiplicity zero, an endpoint degeneracy (Astra 3).
7. The mass-costate identity `int T Q_mt dt = m(0) lambda_m(0) - m(t_f) lambda_m(t_f)` and `d lambda_m/dt = -T |lambda_v| / m^2` (Astra 6).
8. Cite the theorem (fork A 6, Astra).

## Q3. Generalisation to any orbit pair

The two inventories agree and are complementary. The short version:

- **The thrust-ladder pipeline is already pair-generic.** `DPO_tulip`, `HALO_tulip`, `HALO_HALO` and the GTO campaign all go through `thrust_ladder_library` with `depFamily/depParams` + `arrFamily/arrParams` resolved by `ladder_endpoints` / `get_family_orbit` (fork B section 4).
- **The phase-torus pipeline is not.** Its one root is `arclength_arrival.m:63-86`: `ob` is built from `tauDRO/NpTulip/pmTulip` only, so `ladder_endpoints` always takes its legacy branch, and `B.problem` enumerates those three fields. Every identity check downstream (`build_ribs:66`, `rib_validate:89`, `package_phase_catalog:106`, `run_costate_library:254-262`, `sheet_to_catalog_file:71`, `fill_holes_direct:97-98`, `run_phase_torus:157-160, 463, 560-561`) is a copy of that field list; the tulip period formula appears six times; the family names are literals in `sheet_to_catalog_file:177-178`, `package_phase_catalog:47, 83`, `run_phase_torus:362-363`; the `70mN` and `dro_tulip` file names in five places; `dro_tulip_library` seeds by default.
- **The abstraction both reviewers propose is the same object:** one `pair` struct (`dep`, `arr`, each with `family`, `params`, `state(s)`, `dstate(s)`, `period`, a `kind` and a `wrap` flag; `model` with mu, lStar, tStar; one identity string) stored as `B.problem` and compared by identity string everywhere a field list is compared today. Effort ranking (fork B section 3, Astra "refactor order"): (1) `make_orbit_pair` + identity string + one phase-matching contract, small; (2) decouple chart setup from anchor loading (`physicsOnly`), small and immediately unblocks single transfers and non-default phases; (3) `arclength_arrival` takes `pair`, `sheet_to_catalog_file`/`package_phase_catalog` name and describe from it, `run_costate_library`/`run_phase_torus` section 0 take family+params for any provider family, medium; (4) artifact schemas and resume keys bound to root IDs and policy hashes, medium-large; (5) serialized campaign spec instead of generated MATLAB source, medium.
- **Two things the abstraction does not solve, and must not hide.** (i) `get_family_orbit` lacks Pumpkin, LPO, Axial and Cycler (Astra) although `transfer_problem_space.md` lists nine families. (ii) **GTO** (Astra's sharpest Q3 point): `get_family_orbit.m:89-127` builds a closed algebraic locus of rotating-frame states at fixed orientation, not a propagated rotating-frame trajectory. As a DEPARTURE locus that is fine (fork B: ribs only need `stateD(s)` at discrete phases). As an ARRIVAL it is a different problem class: a physically evolving GTO is epoch-dependent, `x_A(s+1) ~= x_A(s)`, the terminal condition becomes `x(t_f) = x_A(t_0 + t_f)` and the free-time condition gains a target-motion term `H(t_f) - lambda_rv(t_f) . xdot_A = 0`; periodic seam forcing, integer-shift crossing search and mod-1 identity are all wrong. The `kind` field ('periodic-flow' | 'closed-locus' | 'epoch-path') exists to keep those apart.
- **Schema:** the catalog is z8-only (`sheet_to_catalog_file` drops `Y`), and every downstream seed is rebuilt by flying z8. Fine at 1-3 revs; the flagship adjudication (bare z8 identifiability at ~40 revs) says a GTO-departure library needs the junction states in the file. Both reviewers flag it; Astra also notes the certificate record exported (z8, coarse conjugate verdict, three hypothesis numbers) cannot show which gate stack an entry passed. Add an entry-indexed `Y` block and a versioned certificate record before any long-transfer pair.

## Ranked top five, all three questions

1. **Audit fails open** (Q1-3): the library-of-record audits do not mean what FINDINGS 70-72 say they mean until re-run fail-closed.
2. **Setup at the shipped operating point** (Q1-1 = driver P0-1): blocks every non-default campaign and is step 1 of Q3.
3. **The optimality certificate is fixed-phase and sampled** (Q2 a-c): say so in the catalog's certificate record now; add the phase-transversality cross-check and the H2/H3 margins next, they are cheap.
4. **Identity by t_f** (Q1-9, driver P1-10): suppresses new families exactly where discovery looks for them.
5. **Rescan loses fold-adjacent crossings** (Q1-4): silent, rare, fixable by persisting the turning point.
