## DOMAIN

Low-thrust orbit transfer in the Circular Restricted Three-Body Problem (CR3BP),
Earth-Moon system, nondimensional units (mass parameter mu, length unit LU = Earth-Moon
distance, time unit TU). Spacecraft: m0 = 150 kg, Isp = 1710 s, thrust rungs 0.5-15 N.
Transfers depart a Distant Retrograde Orbit (DRO) and arrive on a "tulip" orbit.

State: x = [r(3); v(3); m] in the CR3BP rotating frame. Control: thrust direction u
(unit vector) and throttle delta in [0,1].

The pipeline under review is the FIXED-FINAL-TIME objective line:

1. A direct collocation NLP (IPOPT, not in this bundle) solves a fixed-t_f min-ENERGY
   problem, where t_f = gamma * t_f_minTime for a continuation parameter gamma > 1.
2. The NLP duals are harvested as costate seeds.
3. `ms_bvp` (generic multiple shooting) refines those seeds into a true PMP solution of
   the indirect two-point boundary value problem, at FIXED t_f.
4. An epsilon-homotopy continuation deforms the smooth min-energy problem into the
   nonsmooth min-FUEL (bang-bang, L1-in-throttle) problem as eps -> 0.
5. A second-order check (`ms_conjugate_test`) looks for interior conjugate points.
6. Accepted records are packaged into a "catalog" (`catalog_schema`, schema v3).

The min-fuel switching function is the standard one; the smoothed throttle uses the
Bertrand-Epenoy regularization, delta = (1/2)(1 + tanh(-S/eps)) or an equivalent
logistic form -- VERIFY the exact form used and whether it is self-consistent between
the field (`cr3bp_minfuel_pmp`) and the propagator (`cr3bp_minfuel_prop`).

## WHAT TO REVIEW

Everything in the SOURCE section. Prioritize in this order:

### A. PMP / math correctness (highest priority)
- `cr3bp_minfuel_pmp.m` and `cr3bp_minenergy_pmp.m`: are the costate equations
  lambda_dot = -(df/dx)^T lambda correct for the CR3BP? Check the gravity-gradient
  block, the Coriolis/rotating-frame terms (the 2*Omega cross-terms), and the centrifugal
  terms. Check the MASS costate equation and its sign. Check the transversality /
  terminal costate handling at fixed t_f.
- Is the optimal control (primer vector) recovered correctly? u* = -lambda_v/||lambda_v||.
  What happens when ||lambda_v|| -> 0 (singular arc)?
- Is the switching function S correct, including the 1/m and Isp*g0 factors? A common
  error is dropping the mass costate term or getting the sign of S backwards.
- Is the smoothed throttle CONSISTENT with the Hamiltonian being minimized? A smoothing
  applied to the control without the matching term in the running cost gives a control
  that is not the minimizer of the smoothed Hamiltonian -- check this specifically.
- STATE TRANSITION MATRIX: if the variational equations are propagated, does the
  Jacobian used for the STM include the derivative of the SMOOTHED control with respect
  to the state and costate? Omitting d(delta)/d(S) * d(S)/d(z) makes the STM wrong at
  small eps exactly where it matters most, and would silently corrupt both the shooting
  Jacobian and the conjugate-point test.

### B. The conjugate-point test
- `ms_conjugate_test.m`: at FIXED final time the conjugate-point condition is NOT the
  same as the free-time one. The free-time test looks for a singularity of the
  restricted variational flow on the subspace orthogonal to the extremal's own
  direction; the fixed-time test uses the full block. Which is implemented? Is the
  correct sub-block of the STM used (the d(delta x_f)/d(delta lambda_0) block)?
- Is a "conjugate point" declared from a sign change of a determinant, a rank drop, or a
  singular value crossing? Determinant sign changes are numerically fragile when the
  block is badly scaled -- check for scaling and for missed DOUBLE crossings (a
  determinant that dips through zero and back between sample times is missed entirely by
  a sign test on a coarse grid).
- Does the test EXCLUDE the endpoints correctly? A conjugate point exactly at t_f is a
  boundary case, not an interior refutation.

### C. Continuation / homotopy logic
- `ms_minfuel.m`, `run_minfuel_race.m`, `run_minfuel_grid.m`: is the eps ladder
  monotone, and is the step-back / retry logic correct on failure? Are converged
  solutions at eps_k actually reused as the seed for eps_{k+1}, or is something reset?
- The "gamma-continuation rescue" claim: descend eps first, THEN move gamma. Does the
  code as written actually do that, or does it also change eps when gamma moves?
- Is the acceptance gate applied at the FINAL eps or at some intermediate eps? Accepting
  at a loose eps and reporting it as a min-fuel solution would be a significant overclaim.

### D. Catalog schema / data integrity
- `catalog_schema.m` (v3): is v1/v2 backward compatibility actually preserved, or do the
  new required fields silently break old catalogs? Are the derived quantities
  (mf_frac, deltav_from_mf) computed with the right rocket equation and the right units?
  deltav = Isp*g0*log(m0/mf) -- check g0's units and whether Isp is in seconds.
- `build_minfuel_catalog.m`: are conjugate verdicts and junction states written for every
  entry, or can an entry be packaged with a missing/stale verdict?

### E. Numerical robustness
- Integration tolerances vs. the eps of the homotopy: is the ODE tolerance tightened as
  eps shrinks? A fixed RelTol=1e-10 with eps=1e-3 may be fine; the reverse is not.
- Division by near-zero: ||lambda_v||, mass, eps itself.
- tanh/exp overflow at small eps (S/eps can be enormous) -- is it guarded?
- Event detection / discontinuity handling across switches.

### F. MATLAB code quality
- Function headers must document purpose, INPUTS with sizes, OUTPUTS with sizes, and
  REFERENCES where the math comes from. Flag any that do not.
- House rules: never use `i` or `j` as loop indices (imaginary unit). Pre-allocate.
  Vectorize where it matters.
- Off-by-one and indexing errors, especially in the multiple-shooting node bookkeeping
  in `ms_bvp.m` (segment k -> node k+1 mapping is a classic source of silent bugs).
